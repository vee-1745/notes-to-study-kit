notes-to-study-kit

Turn your notes into a study kit.

Upload photos of your notes. Get a summary, flashcards and a quiz, in English, Hindi or Marathi.

01 / Upload

Choose photos or drop them here Up to 4 images, 6 MB each. Clear, well-lit photos work best.

02 / Settings

Fast suits most pages. Choose Best quality for messy handwriting or dense pages. It is a little slower.
